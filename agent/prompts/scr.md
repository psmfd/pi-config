---
name: scr
description: Simplify, compress, and restate your previous response without losing information.
---

Simplify and compress your previous response — your most recent assistant
turn, all its text segments — then restate it. Cut every sentence that
does not change what the operator knows or does. Keep all facts, numbers,
and caveats that alter a decision. Do not add new content. If there is no
previous response, say so.
