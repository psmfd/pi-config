Re-explain your previous response — your most recent assistant turn — as
if to a smart 18-year-old new to this domain: plain language, jargon
expanded or replaced on first use, shorter overall. Keep the conclusions,
numbers, and caveats exact, and keep any question the operator must answer
clearly marked as needing their answer — simplify the language, never the
facts. If there is no previous response, say so.
