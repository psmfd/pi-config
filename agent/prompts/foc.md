name: foc
description: Boil your previous response down to the single thing that matters most.
---

Focus. From your previous response — your most recent assistant turn —
what is the true signal: the one thing that matters most, and the single
action or decision it points to? Answer with that alone, keeping any
caveat that changes whether or when the operator acts on it. If something
genuinely competes for first place, name it in one line, not a section.
If there is no previous response, say so.
